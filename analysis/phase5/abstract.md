# Abstract (standalone)

The FLEX-PSI69 experimental table records 274 droplet-combustion tests flown
on the International Space Station — 157 with methanol and 117 with n-heptane
("Heptane") — spanning March 2009 to December 2011. This paper reports a
comparative analysis of the recordable quantities — documented outcome
labels, burn duration, initial droplet diameter, visible flame extinction
diameter, and ambient conditions — and states explicitly which comparisons
the data support and which they do not. Three findings are supported as
descriptive differences: recorded outcome-label composition (Extinction
80.3% of methanol records vs 51.3% of heptane records; Freeman–Halton exact
test p = 1.75×10⁻⁶, Cramér's V = 0.3099), burn duration (medians 12.10 s vs
7.50 s; Mann–Whitney U = 4838, p = 1.17×10⁻⁹ by asymptotic approximation,
5×10⁻⁶ by Monte Carlo permutation; rank-biserial r = 0.438;
heptane-minus-methanol median difference −4.6 s, 95% bootstrap CI −7.8 to
−2.9), and — with a stated caveat — nothing in initial droplet diameter,
whose fuel contrast is sensitive to the handling of a duplicated identifier
(p = 0.0623 as reported; p = 0.0450 with either colliding record removed;
p = 0.123 with anomalous records screened). Visible flame extinction
diameter is reported for 197 of 274 records, with 77 missing and eight
recorded 0 mm values retained as measurement-floor candidates rather than
physical zeros. Of ten oxygen-matched CO₂/He diluent-substitution cells,
only four are fully comparable on the documented criteria; all nine records
above 800 mmHg belong to a single CO₂-diluted family, so no independent
pressure effect is identified. A recorded burning-rate column contains 258
values but its definition and unit are unresolved in all available
documentation, and the d²-law-constant reading is contradicted by the data
itself; it is therefore reported as an unresolved scalar without physical
interpretation. Finally, experimental independence is not established — the
documented limiting-oxygen-index procedure is sequential and adaptive,
records cluster into 81 series, and one identifier is duplicated — so every
p-value in this paper is conditional on an assumption the design does not
establish. No causal, mechanistic, or extrapolative physical claim is made.
