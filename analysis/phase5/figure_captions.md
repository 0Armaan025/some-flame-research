# Figure Captions

All six figures are generated by the Phase 4A pipeline
(`analysis/phase4a/figures/`, byte-reproducible from
`analysis/phase4a/run_manifest.json`); the Phase 4A outputs are referenced,
unmodified. Panel-level sample sizes are printed on the figures; count axes
start at zero.

**Figure 1 — Recorded outcome-label composition by fuel.**
(`fig01_outcome_distribution.png`; data: `outcome_comparison.csv`.)
Grouped bars of the recorded `Test end` labels — Extinction, Disruption,
Completion — for methanol (n = 157) and heptane (n = 117), with counts and
within-fuel percentages on each bar (methanol: 126/80%, 23/15%, 8/5%;
heptane: 60/51%, 38/32%, 19/16%). What it demonstrates: the recorded label
composition differs strongly by fuel (Freeman–Halton p = 1.75×10⁻⁶; Cramér's
V = 0.3099). What it does not demonstrate: mechanism — labels are recorded
observations, "Disruption" is semantically unverified, and Extinction denotes
visible flame extinction, not chemical extinction.

**Figure 2 — Burn-duration distributions and summaries by fuel.**
(`fig02_burn_duration.png`; data: `duration_comparison.csv`.)
Left: histograms of exact recorded burn times (methanol n = 155, heptane
n = 111; the two `~`-approximate records excluded and listed separately).
Right: box-plus-jitter summaries with medians (12.10 s vs 7.50 s) and IQRs
(7.85–17.90 s vs 3.30–11.25 s), y-axis from zero. What it demonstrates: a
large descriptive separation of duration distributions (U = 4838,
p = 1.17×10⁻⁹; r = 0.438; median difference −4.6 s, 95% CI −7.8 to −2.9 s).
What it does not demonstrate: a combustion mechanism — duration is a recorded
scalar, condition programmes differ between fuels, and per-run temperature is
unknown for 266 records.

**Figure 3 — Initial droplet diameter by fuel with the duplicated-identifier
records marked.** (`fig03_initial_diameter.png`; data:
`diameter_comparison.csv`, `sensitivity_analysis.csv`.)
Box-plus-jitter summaries (methanol n = 154, median 3.05 mm; heptane n = 108,
median 2.92 mm); the two `193F001` collision records are ringed. What it
demonstrates: the as-reported contrast is inconclusive (p = 0.0623), and the
conclusion is record-handling dependent — removing either colliding record
gives p = 0.0450, anomaly screening gives p = 0.123, and the series-level
contrast (4 series) gives p = 0.312. What it does not demonstrate: a
confirmed fuel difference in initial diameter.

**Figure 4 — Visible flame extinction diameter by fuel within each recorded
outcome.** (`fig04_extinction_diameter.png`; data:
`extinction_diameter_assessment.csv`.)
Box-plus-jitter summaries for methanol and heptane within Extinction,
Disruption, and Completion (e.g. Extinction: methanol n = 103, heptane
n = 56); recorded 0 mm values are marked with a black × and retained, not
dropped. What it demonstrates: extinction-diameter reporting is limited —
197 of 274 records measured, 77 missing, eight measurement-floor zeros — and
distributions differ by fuel and outcome as recorded. What it does not
demonstrate: physical extinction diameters, chemical extinction, or the
meaning of a recorded zero.

**Figure 5 — Matched-oxygen CO₂/He substitution cells and their
comparability class.** (`fig05_condition_cells.png`; data:
`condition_comparison.csv`.)
Per-side record counts (solid = CO₂-substituted, pale = He-substituted) for
the ten oxygen-matched cells, coloured by comparability class: green = fully
comparable (O₂ = 0.16, 0.17, 0.18, 0.24; identical reported N₂ balances,
shared pressure band, ≥3 per side), orange = partial (O₂ = 0.20, 0.21, 0.23;
N₂ balances differ), grey = rejected (O₂ = 0.14, 0.15, 0.19; a side below
the minimum count). What it demonstrates: only four cells support a primary
diluent comparison, and sparsity (e.g. 4|3, 16|3) qualifies even those. What
it does not demonstrate: any pooled diluent effect.

**Figure 6 — Pressure versus oxygen with the high-pressure cluster marked.**
(`fig06_pressure_confounding.png`; data: `condition_comparison.csv`.)
Scatter of reported ambient pressure (mmHg) against initial O₂ mole fraction
by fuel; the nine records above 800 mmHg are marked ×. What it demonstrates:
those nine records all belong to the single family
O₂ = 0.21/N₂ = 0.09/CO₂ = 0.70/He = 0, so pressure and diluent are
confounded above 800 mmHg. What it does not demonstrate: an independent
pressure effect anywhere in the dataset.
